### Slide 6: Proposed Project — Title & Introduction
* **Project Title:** *Mobile Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) to Cuffless Blood Pressure Estimation*
* **Clinical Motivation & Context:**
  * Hypertension is a major global health risk, often termed the "silent killer" due to lack of early symptoms.
  * Conventional blood pressure measurement relies on inflatable arm cuffs (sphygmomanometers), which are intermittent, bulky, and unsuitable for continuous monitoring.
* **Proposed Concept:**
  * Explore the feasibility of using standard smartphone RGB front cameras to detect subtle, imperceptible transcutaneous color variations caused by blood volume pulses (remote Photoplethysmography / rPPG).
  * Map extracted optical pulse signals to estimated Systolic (SBP) and Diastolic (DBP) blood pressure values using deep regression models.

---

### Slide 7: Objectives & Scope of the Proposed Project
* **Core Objectives:**
  1. **Optical Pulse Extraction:** Capture facial video via smartphone camera and extract clean rPPG pulse waveforms using ROI localization and chrominance/POS algorithms.
  2. **Physiological Signal Filtering:** Apply digital filtering techniques (including bandpass and Savitzky-Golay / Wavelet smoothing) to isolate heart pulse harmonics (0.75–2.5 Hz).
  3. **Deep Learning Regression:** Develop and evaluate a deep neural network (combining 1D-CNN, Recurrent/GRU layers, and Attention) to estimate SBP and DBP.
  4. **Calibration & Personalization Study:** Investigate the impact of single-point calibration to account for individual arterial stiffness and baseline physiological variations.
  5. **Feasibility on Edge Mobile Devices:** Assess prototype inference feasibility for on-device, privacy-preserving mobile execution using lightweight models.

---

### Slide 8: Slide 9: Methodology / Approach of the Proposed Project
* **Slide Purpose:** Present the end-to-end 5-stage technical acquisition and inference pipeline.
* **5-Stage Pipeline Breakdown:**
  * **Stage 0 (Sensor Lock):** Camera2 API explicit sensor lock (ISO gain and fixed exposure) to eliminate ambient auto-exposure gain oscillations.
  * **Stage 1 (Facial Landmark ROI Tracking):** Facial landmark tracking (forehead and cheek regions) to isolate transcutaneous skin pixels.
  * **Stage 2 (Chrominance Subspace - POS):** Color subspace projection (Plane-Orthogonal-to-Skin) to separate pulse signals from specular ambient reflections (8.9 dB SNR).
  * **Stage 3 (Physiological DSP & Filtering):** Temporal resampling (125 Hz) via PCHIP, Butterworth bandpass (0.75–2.5 Hz), and polynomial smoothing (**Savitzky-Golay filter**).
  * **Stage 4 (Deep Sequence Regression):** Sequence-to-value regression model extracting morphological pulse features to predict SBP and DBP values.

### Slide 9: Slide 10: Proposed Project Work Details & Live Demonstration
* **Slide Purpose:** Detail the neural network architecture in TensorFlow and provide a dedicated transition touchpoint for live mobile demonstration.
* **Architecture Specifications (MODEL-06-SepHead):**
  * **Input Tensor:** $1 \times 1250$ standardized BVP pulse samples (10s @ 125 Hz) + demographic metadata.
